# Hollywood Wind

A cinematic website concept for a boutique representation and partnerships brand across entertainment, sports, NIL, and culture. The layout takes structural inspiration from [Parametric Portfolio Associates](https://www.parametricportfolio.com/)—a concise value proposition, clear audience paths, specialty areas, editorial perspective, and a direct inquiry route—then uses a distinct Hollywood Wind identity and visual language.

## Preview

After GitHub Pages is enabled, the public preview will be available at:

`https://blacksheepbetting.github.io/hollywoodwind/`

## Site notes

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; no build step or third-party runtime dependencies.
- Responsive navigation and audience tabs are interactive.
- The inquiry form is a local preview only. It does not send or store submissions.
- The hero image is an original generated concept image with anonymous figures. It does not depict Hollywood Wind clients.
- Search indexing is disabled for the proposal preview.

## Strategic concerns

- The concept presents entertainment representation, sports representation, NIL opportunities, athlete marketing, and brand partnerships. Confirm the exact services and positioning before launch.
- No roster, deal history, client logo, credential, result, or contact detail has been invented or displayed.

## Missing information

- Approved logo and brand assets.
- Confirmed roster categories and authorized names, biographies, headshots, and credits.
- Verified services, representation scope, and any required credentials or disclosures.
- Approved contact email, phone, social accounts, and inquiry routing.
- Approved examples, testimonials, press mentions, and brand-partnership case studies, if available.

## Scope risks

- The site is a presentation concept. Its inquiry form is not connected to an inbox or CRM.
- NIL language, athlete eligibility, representation arrangements, and contract processes require owner review before public launch.
- The hero illustration is generated concept imagery and should be replaced if Hollywood Wind prefers approved photography.

Review the service mix and provide the real brand assets, contact route, and approved roster/partnership information. Then replace the demo form behavior with the approved inquiry destination.
